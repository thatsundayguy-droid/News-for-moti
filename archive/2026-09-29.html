Consumer Trends — India & Global

India's consumer boom is moving beyond the top 5 cities

A striking insight from the World Economic Forum: 93% of India's urban consumer class growth over the next 15 years will occur outside Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, and Hyderabad. By 2035, India will have ~499 cities where three-quarters of residents qualify as consumers (spending $13+ per day). By 2040, 149 cities will have half-a-million consumers each.

  • Middle and affluent classes will rise from 80% of spending in 2026 to 93% by 2036.
  • Brands relying on metro-first strategies face a reckoning: distributed operations across hundreds of smaller cities are now table stakes.
  • India's consumption geography looks increasingly like China's a decade ago.

CFA Corner
Concept of the Day — Gordon Growth Model

The Gordon Growth Model (GGM) -- also called the Dividend Discount Model -- values a stock based on a perpetually growing stream of future dividends. It is the cornerstone of intrinsic value analysis for dividend-paying equities.

V = D⊂1; ÷ (r − g)

Where: D⊂1; = next year's expected dividend per share  |  r = required rate of return  |  g = constant dividend growth rate

Key assumption: g must be less than r, and the model assumes dividends grow at a constant rate forever -- making it most useful for mature, stable companies in steady-state industries. For high-growth or cyclical firms, multi-stage DDM models are preferred.
