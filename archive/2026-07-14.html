The Iran War  ·  Operation Epic Fury
Timeline of the Conflict  ·  Feb – July 2026
Britannica / Wikipedia / Time / Reuters  ·  Compiled July 14, 2026
Feb 28, 2026 — Operation Epic Fury Begins
US and Israeli forces launch coordinated strikes on Iranian military, nuclear and command-control infrastructure. Supreme Leader Khamenei killed in opening strikes alongside IRGC commander. Iran retaliates within hours.
Feb 28 – Mar 2026 — Strait of Hormuz Closed
IRGC closes the Strait to US/Israel-allied shipping. Global oil prices spike. India confirmed as a "friendly nation" allowing select tanker passage.
March 2026 — Regional Escalation
Iran retaliates via proxy forces across Iraq, Syria, Lebanon and Yemen. US strikes expand. Humanitarian concerns after school hit in Minab, southern Iran.
April 2026 — Fragmented Ceasefire
Conditional ceasefire agreed — frequently violated. US maintains naval Gulf presence; Iran asserts Hormuz toll rights. Multiple lapse-and-renew cycles.
June 17, 2026 — Versailles MoU
US-Iran MoU signed — Iran reaffirms non-pursuit of nuclear weapons; 60-day timeline for a nuclear deal; Strait reopened to commercial vessels.
June 27-29, 2026 — Ceasefire Cracks
Iran strikes US sites in Kuwait and Bahrain. US retaliates. Trump threatens "annihilation." New pause agreed; Strait reopened again.
July 6-7, 2026 — Commercial Vessels Fired On
Iran fires on three commercial vessels in the Strait. US strikes Iranian coastal radar sites. Doha talks collapse.
July 13, 2026 — Hormuz Re-Closed
Trump reinstates the "Iranian Blockade," positioning US as "Guardian of the Hormuz Strait" with a proposed 20% cargo levy. Iran closes Strait. Crude surges 3%+; Sensex gaps down 700 pts before recovering.

CFA Prep  ·  Formula of the Day
Today's Formula — Receivables Turnover & Days Sales Outstanding
Receivables Turnover = Revenue ÷ Average Accounts Receivable
Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) = 365 ÷ Receivables Turnover
Average AR = (Beginning AR + Ending AR) ÷ 2
Exam tip: Use Revenue (not COGS) in the numerator — the reverse of Inventory Turnover. Lower DSO = faster collections = stronger liquidity. A rising DSO trend signals deteriorating receivables quality.
