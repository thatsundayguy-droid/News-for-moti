CFA Prep  ·  Formula of the Day
Today's Formula — Price-to-Earnings (Justified P/E)
Justified P/E = (Dividend Payout Ratio) ÷ (Required Return − Growth Rate)
P/E = D1/E1 ÷ (r − g)  |  where D1/E1 = payout ratio, r = required return on equity, g = sustainable dividend growth rate
Also written as: P/E = (1 − b) ÷ (r − ROE × b)  |  where b = retention ratio
Exam tip: A higher justified P/E is supported by a higher payout ratio, lower required return, or higher growth. Rising oil prices increase required return (via risk premium) and compress justified P/E — exactly what's happening to Indian equities right now.
