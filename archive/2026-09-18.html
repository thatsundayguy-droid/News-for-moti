CFA Prep  ·  Formula of the Day
Today's Formula — Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)
WACC = (E/V) × Re + (D/V) × Rd × (1 − T)
E = market value of equity  |  D = market value of debt  |  V = E + D (total capital)
Re = cost of equity  |  Rd = pre-tax cost of debt  |  T = corporate tax rate
Exam tip: Rising long-duration Treasury yields (as seen this week) directly increase Re and Rd, lifting WACC and compressing intrinsic valuations — the mechanism that links bond market stress to equity market selloffs. The debt tax shield (1 − T) means more leverage reduces WACC, but only up to the point where financial distress risk rises.
