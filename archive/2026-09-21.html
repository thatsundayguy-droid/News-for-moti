CFA Prep  ·  Formula of the Day
Today's Formula -- Free Cash Flow to the Firm (FCFF)
FCFF = EBIT x (1 - T) + D&A - delta WC - Capex
EBIT = earnings before interest and tax  |  T = tax rate  |  D&A = depreciation and amortisation
delta WC = change in working capital (increase = cash outflow)  |  Capex = capital expenditure
Also expressed as: FCFF = Net Income + D&A + Interest x (1-T) - delta WC - Capex
Exam tip: FCFF is pre-debt-service (available to both equity and debt holders), while FCFE is post-debt. When a firm carries significant debt and rates are rising (as now), the gap between FCFF and FCFE widens -- a heavily levered firm may have healthy FCFF but negative FCFE after debt obligations.
