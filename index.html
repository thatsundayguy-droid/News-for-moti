CFA Prep  ·  Formula of the Day
Today's Formula — Inventory Turnover & Days of Inventory on Hand
Inventory Turnover = COGS ÷ Average Inventory
Days of Inventory on Hand (DOH) = 365 ÷ Inventory Turnover
Average Inventory = (Beginning Inventory + Ending Inventory) ÷ 2
Exam tip: Always use COGS (not Revenue) in the numerator — using Revenue overstates turnover. Lower DOH means faster inventory cycle; higher DOH may signal slow-moving stock or build-up risk.
