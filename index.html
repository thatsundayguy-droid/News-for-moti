The Iran War  ·  Operation Epic Fury
Timeline of the Conflict  ·  February – July 2026
Britannica / Wikipedia / Time / Reuters  ·  Compiled July 14, 2026
Feb 28, 2026 — Operation Epic Fury Begins
US and Israeli forces launch coordinated strikes on Iranian military, nuclear and command-control infrastructure. Supreme Leader Khamenei killed in opening strikes alongside IRGC commander and armed forces chief. Iran retaliates within hours with drones and ballistic missiles.
Feb 28 – Mar 2026 — Strait of Hormuz Closed
IRGC announces closure of the Strait of Hormuz to US- and Israel-allied shipping. Global oil prices spike sharply. India confirms "friendly nation" status allows passage for select tankers.
March 2026 — Regional Escalation
Iran retaliates via proxy forces across Iraq, Syria, Lebanon and Yemen. US strikes expand to missile storage, radar and drone sites. Humanitarian concerns mount after school hit in Minab, southern Iran.
April 2026 — Fragmented Ceasefire
Fragmented ceasefire agreed — heavily conditioned, frequently violated. US maintains naval presence in the Gulf; Iran asserts Hormuz toll rights. Multiple lapse-and-renew cycles over April–May.
June 17, 2026 — Versailles MoU
US-Iran Memorandum of Understanding signed in Versailles — reaffirms Iranian non-pursuit of nuclear weapons; sets 60-day timeline for nuclear deal negotiations; reopens Strait to commercial vessels. Iran President Pezeshkian participates.
June 27-29, 2026 — Ceasefire Cracks
Iran launches missiles and drones at US military sites in Kuwait and Bahrain over the weekend. US responds with fresh strikes. Trump threatens "annihilation." New tit-for-tat pause agreed, Hormuz reopened.
July 6–7, 2026 — Iran Fires on Commercial Vessels
Iran fires on three commercial vessels in the Strait, testing limits of the MoU. US responds with strikes on Iranian coastal radar sites. Doha talks collapse — Iran denies meeting was scheduled.
July 13, 2026 — Hormuz Re-Closed
Trump declares the US is reinstating the "Iranian Blockade," positioning America as "Guardian of the Hormuz Strait" and proposing a 20% levy on cargo transiting under US protection. Iran closes Strait again. Oil spikes at open; Sensex gaps down 700 pts before recovering.

CFA Prep  ·  Formula of the Day
Today's Formula — Receivables Turnover & Days of Sales Outstanding
Receivables Turnover = Revenue ÷ Average Accounts Receivable
Days of Sales Outstanding (DSO) = 365 ÷ Receivables Turnover
Average AR = (Beginning AR + Ending AR) ÷ 2
Exam tip: Lower DSO = faster collections = better liquidity. Use Revenue (not COGS) in the numerator — the reverse of Inventory Turnover where COGS is correct.
