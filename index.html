CFA Prep  ·  Formula of the Day
Today's Formula — Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE)
FCFE = Net Income + Depreciation − ΔWorking Capital − Capex + Net Borrowing
Net Borrowing = new debt issued − debt repaid  |  ΔWC = increase in working capital (cash outflow)
Also: FCFE = CFO − Capex + Net Borrowing  (from the cash flow statement)
Exam tip: FCFE is the cash available to equity holders after debt obligations are met — it differs from FCFF which is pre-debt-service. If a company has FCFE consistently below dividends paid, it is either drawing down cash or borrowing to fund payouts — a red flag in valuation.
