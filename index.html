CFA Prep  ·  Formula of the Day
Today's Formula — DuPont 3-Factor ROE Decomposition
ROE = Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier
Net Profit Margin = Net Income ÷ Revenue  |  Asset Turnover = Revenue ÷ Average Total Assets
Equity Multiplier = Average Total Assets ÷ Average Shareholders' Equity  (= 1 + Debt-to-Equity)
Exam tip: DuPont disaggregates ROE into profitability, efficiency and leverage — if ROE is high due to a high equity multiplier (heavy leverage), it is less durable than the same ROE achieved through margin or turnover improvement. Compare the three components across companies, not just the headline ROE.
