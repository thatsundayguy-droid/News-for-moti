CFA Prep  ·  Formula of the Day
Today's Formula -- Single-Factor ROE
ROE = Net Income / Average Shareholders' Equity
Average Equity = (Beginning Equity + Ending Equity) / 2
Also expressed as: ROE = EPS / Book Value per Share
Exam tip: ROE alone is an incomplete metric -- a firm can boost ROE by taking on more debt (raising the equity multiplier in DuPont) without any real improvement in profitability or efficiency. Always decompose ROE via DuPont before drawing conclusions. In a rising-rate environment, highly levered firms may see ROE fall even if net margins hold, because interest costs erode net income.
